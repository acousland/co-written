Welcome to Co-written

1. Drag Co-written into Applications and open it.
2. The app starts silently. Look for the quotation-mark icon in your menu bar.
3. Open Settings and enable Accessibility if you want to analyse selections across apps.
4. Select text, then press Shift–Command–L to analyse it and open the dropdown.

In menu-bar-only mode, selections are read only when you press Shift–Command–L.
The dropdown shows icons and short feature labels on one page. Clicking the icon
opens your last results without reading selected text. Choose Full app for detail.
While the full window is open, mouse selections in other apps update the analysis.
Closing, hiding or minimising the full window stops watching selections.
You can also use an app's Services menu → Analyse with Co-written, or paste a passage.
Some applications do not expose their selected text to macOS Accessibility.

The local analysis estimates grammatical voice, point of view, formality, reading ease,
tone cues, sentence rhythm, repetition, and possible ways to simplify a passage.
Style estimates support English. Results describe patterns rather than prove errors.
Short passages produce less reliable estimates. Your audience and purpose matter.

AI is included by default for requested reviews and settled mouse selections in
the full app, once you add your own OpenAI
API key in Settings and allow sharing. Your key stays in this Mac's Keychain. Saving
a key or changing settings never sends an existing passage. Mouse-triggered AI
requests are spaced by at least 15 seconds. Reviewed passages are
sent directly to OpenAI and usage is billed to your account; provider retention
rules apply. Turn off AI in Settings to keep analysis local. No separate server is
required and no shared key is bundled in the download.
An optional shared-service mode is available for owner-funded access.

The app keeps only the current passage in memory and has no writing history or telemetry.
Use Clear, request a new passage, or quit to discard it. Password managers are
excluded by default, and secure fields are skipped.

Updates are signed and checked before installation. Future releases can use small
delta downloads; a full download is available if a delta cannot be used.
