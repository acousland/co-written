# Co-written 0.3.0

- A succinct menu-bar dropdown: icons, short feature labels and cue counts on one page. Its height adapts to the findings. Expand into the full app for evidence and detailed advice.
- Liquid Glass controls on macOS 26+, system colours and light/dark appearance, with native material fallbacks on older Macs. Readable content surfaces keep the writing clear.
- Open the full app to analyse mouse selections in other apps automatically. Closing, hiding or minimising its window stops selection watching and pending AI work; menu-bar-only operation still uses **⇧⌘L**.
- AI stays enabled by default with your own saved key and sharing permission. Mouse-triggered requests wait for stable selections and are spaced by at least 15 seconds. Explicit shortcut requests and retries remain responsive.
- Start silently in the menu bar. Settings and the full window open only when requested, and saved windows are not restored.
- Signed and notarised universal macOS app, DMG and verified Sparkle delta updates.

AI reviews send the requested or settled highlighted passage to your configured provider and may incur API charges. Each user supplies their own key, stored in Keychain. Turn off AI for local analysis. Style cues, including Humanizer patterns, cannot establish authorship. Live OpenAI calls remain unverified without a saved key; regression checks use mock responses.
